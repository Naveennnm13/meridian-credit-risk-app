Methodology & Fairness

Model overview

Built on XGBoost using the UCI Statlog German Credit dataset, with SHAP for per-decision explainability and an LLM layer that translates technical factors into plain-language explanations, the kind required for adverse action notices in regulated lending.

Fairness correction

Personal status (which conflates gender and marital status in the source data) and foreign worker status were removed from the feature set after an audit showed disparate approval rates across these groups with no accuracy benefit. Removing them eliminated the disparity at no cost to performance.

Precision / recall tradeoff

The model prioritizes recall on bad-credit risk over raw accuracy, since a missed default is costlier than a false rejection. Current performance: 67% recall, 53% precision on bad-credit classification, a deliberate, documented tradeoff.

Known limitation

The dataset codes "no credit history / all paid duly" as a negative signal relative to "critical account, other credits existing," a counterintuitive artifact of the original data collection, disclosed here rather than hidden.
